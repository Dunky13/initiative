# Your space

Most of Initiative is organised by community and initiative, which is excellent right up until you belong to four of them and simply want to know what you're supposed to be doing today.

That's what **your space** is for: personal views that gather *your* things from across **every** community at once.

You've already been in it. Signing in puts you here, and the **Initiative logo** in the **top-left corner** — above your communities on the rail — brings you back from wherever you've got to. The sidebar swaps to your own short list: Home, My Calendar, My Tools, My Messages, My Stats.

## Home

The page signing in drops you on. It's yours to arrange, because people turn up for wildly different reasons: some are here for their own to-do list, some to keep an eye on the scout group, some because their manager assigned them eleven things on a Friday afternoon.

It starts out arranged for whatever you told us when you signed up: your own list gets a planner, a community you run gets its activity and your projects, and a community you joined gets its people and what's coming up. **Getting started** sits near the top with a few first things to do — join a community, add a task, find a face for your profile — and ticks each one off by itself once you've actually done it. Hide it whenever you like. Press **Customize** and you can drag widgets about, pull a corner to resize one, open its settings, take it off, or **Add widget** for something else. On a phone they stack one above another, and customizing moves them up and down instead.

| Widget | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Greeting** | Hello, today's date, and how much is overdue or due today, with Yonder reacting to it. Turn him off in its settings if you'd rather he didn't. |
| **Getting started** | A few first things to do, ticked off as you do them. |
| **Focus** | What wants doing now. More on it below. |
| **My tasks** | Everything assigned to you, from every community, as a table or a calendar. Pick a list in its settings and it gets a line for adding a task straight to it. |
| **Coming up** | Events and task dates over the next few days. You pick how many. |
| **My calendar** | The whole of [My Calendar](#my-calendar), filters and all, by month or week. |
| **Recent** | What you opened lately, or what changed lately, across every kind of tool and every community. Narrow it to a few kinds in its settings. |
| **Favorites** | Everything you've [starred](#favourites), from every community. Narrow it to a few kinds in its settings. |
| **Activity** | What people changed lately in one community. |
| **My communities** | Every community you're in, as the card the directory shows, with a way in. |
| **My progress** | Your streak, your week, and how much lands on time. |
| **Clock and timer** | The time here and in up to three other places, or a countdown. Click the time to type a length (`25`, `5:30`), and use the speaker to mute it. A running timer keeps going after you close the tab, and on your phone. |

### Views

Here for more than one reason? Keep more than one home page. While customizing, **+** beside the tabs makes a new view, starting from a blank page or one of a few ready-made ones:

| Layout | For |
| --- | --- |
| *My tasks* | Your list with what's urgent on top. The plainest of the lot. |
| *My day* | Running your own life on it: what's urgent, the days ahead, your streak, then the whole list. |
| *Heads down* | Getting through it: a timer and the few things that need doing now. |
| *Assigned work* | Work that's handed to you: your list, the urgent part, and what's happened to your things. |
| *Community* | Following a club or a space: your communities, what's going on, and what's coming up. |
| *Running the show* | Keeping a community moving: its activity, what you starred, what changed in projects and dashboards, your own list. |

More are in [the marketplace](plugins-and-marketplace.md#home-layouts) — **Browse the marketplace** at the foot of the same picker.

Each view gets a tab: work on one, the scout group on the other, the allotment rota on a third.

Each view can wear a banner, too. While customizing, **Banner** offers every one you have — the ones that come with Initiative and any from [decoration packs](plugins-and-marketplace.md#decoration-packs) — so the drama club's view can have the stage and work can have nothing at all. Give a pack back and its banner comes off the view with it.

The **⋯** next to the tabs renames, copies, moves or deletes the view you're on. Home opens on whichever view you had last, and the address names it, so a view is something you can bookmark. Deleting one takes its widgets with it and nothing else — your communities never notice.

### My tasks

Group it **by date** or **by community**, filter by priority or status, or swap the table for a **calendar**.

If you only ever look at one widget in Initiative for the rest of your life, it's this one.

### Focus

The short list at the top: what actually wants doing. Overdue work always shows, and past that you set how far ahead to look for each priority — so an urgent thing can appear a month out while a low one waits until the day.

**Pin** anything to keep it there whatever those settings say. The thing you promised on the phone. The thing that isn't due for weeks but will comprehensively ruin the week it *is* due.

Tick something off and it stays until tomorrow, struck through, with the count above it going up. The pile shrinking is the point.

## My Tools

Every [tool](tools.md) of every kind that's reached *you*, from every community, in one table. Pick a tool along the top and the table underneath changes. Each row says which community and which initiative it came from, so you can stop playing *which one was that in again*.

A tool you have none of anywhere doesn't appear at all. Never touched a queue? No queue, and no empty table sulking behind it.

"Reached you" means the same thing everywhere in your space: shared with you, shared with a role you hold, or shared with everyone in an initiative you're in. It does **not** mean "everything you could theoretically prise open if you went looking". Even a community admin, who can reach absolutely anything in their community, gets their own work here rather than a 400-row inventory of everybody else's.

Two switches sit above the table:

- **Everything / Made by me** — the second is exactly what it sounds like: things you wrote, regardless of who's been given them since.
- **Communities** — for when you're in five and today you care about one.

The tool, the search, the order, the page and the communities you picked all live in the address bar. So whatever you're looking at is a link, which is a considerably nicer thing to send somebody than "open My Tools and click around a bit".

## My Messages

Your private conversations — two people or a few, end-to-end encrypted, and the only page here whose contents live on your device rather than on the server.

Beside them is everyone you can reach. Unread first, then the people you starred, then your connections, then everybody else — each group folds away and stays folded. **Requests waiting on you** sit above the lot, connection or message, either direction, so answering one doesn't mean going rummaging in Settings.

Star anyone to keep them near the top. It's completely private and tells them absolutely nothing, which is the correct design for a feature like this.

Nobody in the list yet, or somebody missing from it? The **+** goes and finds them:

- **The people you starred**, first, including anyone you share no community with.
- **Each of your communities**, searchable, twenty at a time with a *Show more* under each.
- **A whole handle, number included** (`sam#1234`) — the one way to reach an account no list of yours will ever show, because they've kept themselves to themselves.

Somebody who takes no messages is shown like anybody else and simply offers nothing to click. They're there, and they're not reachable, and the row says both.

## My Calendar

Your events **and** your dated tasks, from every community, on one calendar.

If you belong to three groups that each quietly believe their thing is the only thing happening in your week, this is the page that settles it.

## My Stats

A friendly look at your own activity:

- **Current streak** — consecutive days you got something done.
- **On-time rate** — how often you finish before the due date.
- **Tasks completed** — this week and all-time.
- **Velocity** — how much you take on versus how much you finish.
- An **activity heatmap** of the past year.

It's only for you. Nobody else can see it, there's no leaderboard, nobody is being ranked against anybody, and no manager is being sent a summary.

It exists because watching a streak build is quite nice, and that turns out to be reason enough.

## Favourites

Anything can have a star: a project, a file, the tea rota, the counter of how many raffle tickets are left. The star sits beside its name at the top of its page, and on its card or row in a list.

Starred things go in **Favorites** at the top of the sidebar, for that community, and in the **Favorites** widget on Home, from all of them. A star is yours alone. Starring the committee's minutes doesn't pin them to anybody else's screen, which is probably for the best.

Leave a community and your stars there go with you.

## Following

A star keeps a thing close. Following keeps an eye on it. Follow the fete project and every stall booking, rota swap and increasingly tense comment about the gazebo rises in the activity feed on the [community front page](communities.md#the-community-front-page). Follow one task, one event or one page of the handbook, and just that rises.

Look for the radar:

| To follow | It's |
|---|---|
| A tool | Beside the star, next to its name. Following a tool covers everything inside it. |
| A task, an event or a queue item | With the other buttons at the top of it. |
| A picture | In the viewer, beside **Details**. |
| A wiki | Beside its name at the top of the wiki's sidebar. Each page has its own, beside the wiki's star. |
| A counter | In its menu. |

Following changes the order of your feed and nothing else. Nobody gets told, nothing pings you, and what you follow is yours alone. It lifts things up the list rather than pinning them to the top, so a busy morning can still outrank the thing you followed last week. Leave a community and your follows there go with you.
